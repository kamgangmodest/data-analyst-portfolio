# Business Insights & Recommendations

This project uses **synthetic operations data** created specifically for portfolio demonstration.

## Executive Summary
- **Received:** 1,000
- **Completed:** 913
- **Open Backlog:** 87
- **On-Time Completion:** 87.4%
- **Average Closure Time:** 31.3 hours
- **Reopen Rate:** 6.2%
- **Exception Rate:** 8.2%

## What Leadership Should Notice

### 1. Overall SLA performance needs attention
The organization completed **87.4%** of finished work on time. Teams below a 90% threshold are: **South, West**.

### 2. West is the highest-priority team for intervention
West has the lowest on-time completion rate at **81.3%**. Management should investigate workload mix, staffing coverage, escalation patterns, and recurring exceptions.

### 3. Backlog is heavily aged
Of 87 open tickets, **86** are 15+ days old at the year-end snapshot. Old backlog can create downstream SLA failures even if current intake looks stable.

### 4. Reopens are a quality signal
The overall reopen rate is **6.2%**. A rising reopen rate can indicate incomplete resolution, unclear work instructions, or quality problems even before SLA performance deteriorates.

### 5. Leading indicators should be monitored before monthly SLA closes
Backlog aging, reopen rate, exception rate, and open past-due work are more actionable than waiting for a month-end SLA result.

## Recommended Actions
1. Establish a weekly at-risk backlog review for items approaching due date.
2. Create a team-level alert when on-time performance falls below 90%.
3. Track reopen and exception rates alongside throughput to prevent quality tradeoffs.
4. Prioritize the 15+ day backlog with an ownership and escalation workflow.
5. Compare workload and priority mix across teams before attributing performance differences only to productivity.
