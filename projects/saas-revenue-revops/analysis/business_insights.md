# Business Insights

## Executive Summary
- Closed-Won ARR: **$2,832,969**
- Open Pipeline: **$1,430,020**
- Win Rate: **44.0%**
- Average Deal Size: **$11,023**
- Average Sales Cycle: **51 days**

## Key Findings
1. **Enterprise** generated the most closed-won ARR.
2. **Event** was the strongest acquisition source by closed-won ARR.
3. Enterprise deals have larger contract values but longer sales cycles.
4. Pipeline quality should be monitored alongside raw pipeline size.
5. Discounting should be reviewed against segment-level win rates and deal size.

## Recommended Actions
- Prioritize high-converting acquisition sources.
- Separate pipeline coverage targets by customer segment.
- Track sales-cycle aging for Enterprise opportunities.
- Review discount levels for deals that do not materially improve close probability.
- Add forecast vs actual ARR to monthly RevOps reviews.
