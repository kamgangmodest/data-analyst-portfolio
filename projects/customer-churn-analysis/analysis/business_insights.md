# Business Insights & Recommendations

This project uses fully synthetic customer data.

## Executive Summary
- Customers: **1,200**
- Churn Rate: **23.9%**
- Monthly Revenue: **$81,281**
- Monthly Revenue at Risk: **$20,907**
- Average Monthly Charge: **$68**
- Average Tenure: **36.5 months**

## Key Findings
1. **Month-to-month** is the highest-risk contract segment at **35.3%** churn.
2. **Electronic check** is the highest-risk payment segment.
3. **Fiber** is the highest-risk internet-service segment.
4. Customers in their first 12 months churn more often than long-tenure customers.
5. Customers without tech support churn more often.

## Recommended Actions
- Target early-tenure month-to-month customers with proactive retention offers.
- Offer contract-conversion incentives to valuable month-to-month customers.
- Prioritize high-charge Fiber customers without tech support.
- Review payment friction among electronic-check customers.
- Track revenue at risk together with churn rate.
