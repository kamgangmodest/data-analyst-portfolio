# Customer Churn & Revenue Analysis

![Customer Churn Dashboard](dashboard/customer_churn_dashboard.svg)

## Project Overview
This end-to-end portfolio project uses **Python, pandas, and BI-style analysis** to identify churn patterns, quantify revenue at risk, and prioritize retention opportunities.

The dataset is fully synthetic and contains **1,200 customers**.

## Business Questions
- Which customer segments are most likely to churn?
- How much monthly revenue is at risk?
- Which contract, payment, service, and tenure patterns are associated with churn?
- Where should retention teams focus first?

## Tools & Skills Demonstrated
**Python • pandas • Data Cleaning • Segmentation • KPI Development • Churn Analysis • Revenue Analysis • Power BI / Tableau Concepts • Business Storytelling**

## Executive KPIs
| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Customers | **1,200** |
| Churn Rate | **23.9%** |
| Monthly Revenue | **$81,281** |
| Monthly Revenue at Risk | **$20,907** |
| Average Monthly Charge | **$68** |
| Average Tenure | **36.5 months** |

## Key Findings
- **Month-to-month** is the highest-risk contract segment.
- **Electronic check** has the highest churn among payment methods.
- **Fiber** is the highest-risk internet-service segment.
- Early-tenure customers show materially higher churn.
- Customers without tech support are more likely to leave.
