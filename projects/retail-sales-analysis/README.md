# Retail Sales & Inventory Performance

## Business Question
How can leadership identify which products, stores, and regions are driving revenue and profit while spotting inventory risks before they affect sales?

## Tools
SQL • Power BI

## Analysis
- Revenue, units sold, gross profit, and margin
- Month-over-month sales trends
- Top and bottom products
- Category and regional performance
- Inventory turnover
- Stockout / low-stock monitoring
- Store ranking
